# Cold-start policy

- **Grace period**: new joiners with insufficient demonstrated evidence are excluded from
  competitive shortlists until at least one demonstrated or certified evidence item exists.
- **Exploration slot** (optional): managers may request a non-scored exploration candidate
  outside the ranked shortlist (UI signal only; not persisted into scoring).
- **Insufficient evidence cohort**: surfaced in Staff UI / fitness audit payload so exclusion
  is visible, not silent.
